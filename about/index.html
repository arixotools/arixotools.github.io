About ARIXO

Smart Tools. Better Life.

What is ARIXO?

ARIXO is a free collection of online tools for everyday tasks. It includes calculators (EMI, GST, loan, BMI, interest and more), date and time tools, text tools, image tools, developer utilities and other handy helpers, all in one place.

Our goals

  1. Simple: open a tool, use it, done. No account and no sign-up.
  2. Fast: the tools run in your browser, so results appear instantly.
  3. Private: most tools process your input on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details, including the one tool that uses a third-party service.
  4. Free: every tool on ARIXO is free to use.

Accuracy

We try to keep every calculator accurate and up to date. Results are estimates meant to help you plan. For financial, tax, legal or medical decisions, please confirm with a qualified professional or the official source.

Feedback

Found a bug, or want a new tool? Tell us on the Contact page. New tools are added regularly.
