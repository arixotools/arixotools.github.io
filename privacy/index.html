Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Overview

ARIXO (https://arixotools.github.io/) provides free online tools. This policy explains what information is and is not collected when you use the site.

Information we collect

ARIXO does not require an account and does not ask you for personal information. Text, numbers, dates and images you enter into most tools are processed in your browser on your own device and are not uploaded to our servers.

QR Code Generator (third-party service)

The QR Code Generator creates the QR image using a third-party service (api.qrserver.com). The text or link you enter is sent to that service to produce the code. Please do not enter passwords or other sensitive information in that tool. The service has its own privacy policy.

Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may collect standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

ARIXO itself does not intentionally set tracking cookies. We may add analytics (for example Google Analytics) to understand how the site is used, and we may show advertisements in the future. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. If we add analytics or ads, this policy will be updated.

External links

ARIXO may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children

ARIXO is a general-audience site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Disclaimer

Calculators and converters provide estimates for general information only and are not financial, tax, legal or medical advice.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact
