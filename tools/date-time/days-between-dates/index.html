About the Days Between Dates

Days Between Dates gives you the exact number of days between any two calendar dates. People use it to count days until a trip, a due date or an anniversary, or to measure how long ago something happened.

How to use

  1. Pick the start date.
  2. Pick the end date.
  3. Click Calculate to get the exact number of days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I count days between two dates by hand?

Count the days left in the first month, add the full months in between, then add the days in the last month. The calculator does this instantly.

Does it include weekends?

Yes. All calendar days are counted, including weekends and holidays.

Can the start date be later than the end date?

Yes. The tool shows the gap between the two dates either way.
