Working Days Calculator

Count working days between two dates, skipping weekends and holidays you list.

About the Working Days Calculator

The Working Days Calculator counts the business days between two dates. Unlike a plain days-between-dates tool, it leaves out weekends and any public holidays you list. Because weekends differ around the world, you can choose Saturday and Sunday, Friday and Saturday (as in Bangladesh and much of the Middle East), or a single weekend day.

How to use the Working Days Calculator

  1. Choose the start and end dates.
  2. Select which days are your weekend.
  3. Tick or untick Include the end date.
  4. Optionally list holidays, one date per line in the format YYYY-MM-DD, then click Calculate.

Where it is useful

  • Estimating project timelines and delivery times.
  • Counting notice periods or leave in working days.
  • Payroll, billing and invoice due dates.
  • Planning exams, courses and study schedules.

Example

October 2026 has 31 days. Counting from 1 to 31 October with Saturday and Sunday as the weekend gives 22 working days, 9 weekend days and no holidays. Add two holiday dates and the result drops to 20.

How holidays are entered

Type each holiday on its own line as year-month-day, for example 2026-12-16. A holiday that falls on a weekend is counted once, as a weekend day.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Public holidays differ by country, state and year, so enter your own list.
  • Decide whether the end date counts, because it can change the answer by a day.
  • If start and end are reversed, the tool swaps them for you.
  • For simply adding days to a date, use the Add or Subtract Days tool.

Frequently asked questions

How do I count working days between two dates?

Count all days in the range and remove weekends and holidays. This tool does it automatically.

What are the weekend days in Bangladesh?

Friday and Saturday are the usual weekend days, so choose that option.

Does it include public holidays by default?

No. You add the holiday dates you want to skip.

Does it include the end date?

Yes by default. You can untick the option to exclude it.

Can I count back from a date?

The calculator counts between two dates. To step back by days, use Add or Subtract Days.
