Hours Between Two Times Calculator

Find the time between a start and end time, with overnight shifts and breaks.

About the Hours Between Two Times Calculator

This time duration calculator tells you how many hours and minutes lie between two clock times. If the end time is earlier than the start time, it treats the span as crossing midnight, which is how night shifts and overnight travel are normally measured. You can also deduct an unpaid break and see the result as decimal hours, which is handy for timesheets and payroll.

How to use the Hours Between Two Times Calculator

  1. Choose the start time and the end time.
  2. Enter any break to deduct, in minutes.
  3. Click Calculate.
  4. Read the duration in hours and minutes, and as decimal hours.

Where it is useful

  • Filling timesheets and calculating paid hours.
  • Working out shift lengths, including night shifts.
  • Measuring travel, study or exercise duration.
  • Billing clients by the hour.

To convert minutes to a decimal, divide by 60. For example, 45 minutes is 0.75 hours and 20 minutes is 0.33 hours.

Why decimal hours matter

Payroll systems often need hours as decimals, not hours and minutes. 7 h 45 min is 7.75 hours, so at an hourly rate of ₹200 the pay is ₹1,550.

Tips and common mistakes

  • If the two times are the same, the tool treats the span as zero.
  • This tool handles spans under 24 hours. For longer spans, use the Days Between Dates tool.
  • Enter times in 24-hour or 12-hour format, depending on your device's time picker.
  • Always deduct unpaid breaks before computing pay.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate hours between two times?

Subtract the start time from the end time. If the end is earlier, add 24 hours. This tool does it for you.

Does it handle night shifts?

Yes. An end time earlier than the start time is treated as the next day.

How do I convert hours and minutes to decimal?

Divide the minutes by 60 and add them to the hours.

Can I deduct a lunch break?

Yes. Enter the break in minutes.

What about durations over 24 hours?

Use the Days Between Dates or Date Calculator tools.
