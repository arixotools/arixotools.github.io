About the Age Difference Calculator

The Age Difference Calculator shows the exact gap between two dates. Use it to compare the ages of two people, siblings or friends, or to find the time between any two dates. Enter both dates and you get the difference in a clear format.

How to use

  1. Enter the first date.
  2. Enter the second date.
  3. Click Calculate to see the age difference.

Frequently asked questions

How is the age difference calculated?

The calculator compares the two dates and shows the exact gap between them.

Does the order of the dates matter?

No. The gap between the two dates is the same whichever one you enter first.

Can I use it for things other than age?

Yes. It works for any two dates, such as the time between two events.
