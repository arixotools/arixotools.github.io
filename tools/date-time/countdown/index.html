About the Countdown Timer

The Countdown Timer counts down to any date and time you choose, such as a birthday, exam, festival or deadline. Pick the target date and time, start the countdown, and the remaining time updates live on the page.

How to use

  1. Select the target date and time.
  2. Click Start Countdown.
  3. Keep the page open to watch the time remaining update.

Frequently asked questions

Does the countdown keep running if I close the page?

No. The timer runs in your browser, so keep the page open. When you return, enter the target date again.

Which time zone does it use?

It uses your device's local time zone.

What if the date has already passed?

Choose a date and time in the future. If the time has already passed, the countdown ends immediately.
