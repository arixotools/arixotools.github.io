About the URL Decoder

The URL Decoder turns percent-encoded text, such as %20 or %26, back into readable characters. It is handy for reading query strings, tracking links and encoded parameters. Decoding happens in your browser.

How to use

  1. Paste the encoded text or URL.
  2. Click Decode.
  3. Copy the readable result.

Frequently asked questions

What does %20 mean?

It is the encoded form of a space character. Other characters have their own percent codes, such as %26 for an ampersand.

Why do URLs need encoding?

URLs can only safely contain certain characters. Encoding replaces the rest with percent codes so browsers and servers read them correctly.

Why does decoding fail?

A stray percent sign that is not followed by two hexadecimal digits makes the input invalid.
