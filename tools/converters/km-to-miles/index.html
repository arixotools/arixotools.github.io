Km to Miles Converter

Convert kilometers to miles or miles to kilometers, with meters, feet and yards.

About the Km to Miles Converter

Road signs and running events use kilometers in India, Bangladesh and most countries, while the USA and the UK use miles for road distance. This converter changes kilometers to miles or miles to kilometers and also shows the same distance in meters, feet and yards, which is useful for running, cycling, driving and travel planning.

How to use the Km to Miles Converter

  1. Enter the distance.
  2. Choose Kilometers to miles or Miles to kilometers.
  3. Click Convert.
  4. Read the main result and the smaller units.

Where it is useful

  • Planning road trips across countries.
  • Comparing running or cycling distances such as a 5K, 10K or half marathon.
  • Reading speed limits and fuel economy in another unit.
  • Checking flight and shipping distances.

Formula

miles = kilometers ÷ 1.609344
kilometers = miles × 1.609344

A mile is exactly 1.609344 km. For a quick estimate, multiply kilometers by 0.62.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Do not use 1.6 for long distances. On 500 km the small rounding adds up to a noticeable error.
  • Nautical miles are different (1.852 km), and this tool does not cover them.
  • Running pace converts the same way: a 6 min/km pace is about 9 min 39 s per mile.
  • Speed in km/h converts to mph with the same factor.

Frequently asked questions

How many miles is 10 km?

10 km is about 6.2137 miles.

How many km is 1 mile?

1 mile is exactly 1.609344 km.

How do I convert km/h to mph?

Divide km/h by 1.609344. For example, 100 km/h is about 62.14 mph.

How many miles is a marathon?

A marathon is 42.195 km, or about 26.22 miles.

Is this the international mile?

Yes. It uses the international mile of 1.609344 km.
