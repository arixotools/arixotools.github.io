CM to Feet and Inches Converter

Convert height between centimeters and feet-and-inches. Fill in one side only.

About the CM to Feet and Inches Converter

Height is written in centimeters in India, Bangladesh and most of the world, but in feet and inches on many forms, passports, dating profiles, sports sites and shopping pages. This converter turns centimeters into feet and inches, or feet and inches back into centimeters. Fill in only one side: centimeters on top, or feet and inches below.

How to use the CM to Feet and Inches Converter

  1. To convert cm, type the height in the Centimeters box and leave the others empty.
  2. To convert feet and inches, type them in the lower boxes and leave centimeters empty.
  3. Click Convert.
  4. Read the result, plus total inches and meters.

Where it is useful

  • Filling forms that ask for height in feet and inches.
  • Comparing heights quoted in different units on sports or profile pages.
  • Buying clothes, beds or furniture listed in inches.
  • Entering height correctly in BMI, calorie and ideal-weight calculators.

Formula

feet = (cm ÷ 2.54) ÷ 12
cm = (feet × 12 + inches) × 2.54

One inch is exactly 2.54 centimeters and one foot is 12 inches (30.48 cm).

Tips and common mistakes

  • Fill in one side at a time. If both are filled, centimeters take priority.
  • Inches can be decimals such as 5.5.
  • Heights near the next inch may look like 6.9 in instead of 7 in. That is rounding, not an error.
  • Use the converted height in the BMI and ideal weight tools for better accuracy.

Frequently asked questions

How many feet is 170 cm?

170 cm is about 5 feet 6.9 inches, or 5.577 feet.

How many cm is 5 feet 7 inches?

5 feet 7 inches is 170.18 cm.

How many cm in one foot?

One foot is exactly 30.48 cm.

How many inches in a cm?

One centimeter is about 0.3937 inches.

Can I enter decimal inches?

Yes. For example 5 feet 6.5 inches is 168.91 cm.
