About the Celsius to Fahrenheit Converter

Most countries measure temperature in Celsius, the United States uses Fahrenheit, and scientists use Kelvin. When you read a weather forecast, a recipe, a fever chart or a science textbook from another country, you need to switch between them quickly. This converter takes a temperature in any of the three scales and shows the other two straight away.

How to use the Celsius to Fahrenheit Converter

  1. Type the temperature.
  2. Choose whether it is in Celsius, Fahrenheit or Kelvin.
  3. Click Convert.
  4. Read the other two scales below.

Where it is useful

  • Reading oven temperatures in American recipes.
  • Understanding weather reports while travelling.
  • Checking body temperature, for example 98.6 °F against 37 °C.
  • Homework and lab work that uses Kelvin.

Formulas

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32
°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
K = °C + 273.15

Tips and common mistakes

  • Choose the correct input unit before converting. A reading of 100 means very different things in °C and °F.
  • Kelvin has no degree symbol and cannot be negative.
  • For a rough mental conversion from Celsius to Fahrenheit, double the number and add 30.
  • Oven temperatures are usually rounded to the nearest 5 or 10 degrees.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9/5 and add 32. For example, 20 °C is 68 °F.

What is 37 °C in Fahrenheit?

37 °C is 98.6 °F, the usual normal body temperature.

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32 and multiply by 5/9.

What is 0 Kelvin?

0 K is absolute zero, which is −273.15 °C.

At what temperature are Celsius and Fahrenheit equal?

They are equal at −40 degrees.
