About the Kg to Lbs Converter

Weight is quoted in kilograms in most of the world and in pounds in the United States and the United Kingdom's everyday talk, so you meet both whenever you read a recipe, a gym plan, a baggage rule or an online product page. This converter changes kilograms to pounds, or pounds to kilograms, in one step and also shows the same weight in grams, ounces and stone.

How to use the Kg to Lbs Converter

  1. Type the weight you have.
  2. Choose Kilograms to pounds or Pounds to kilograms.
  3. Click Convert or press Enter.
  4. Read the main result and the extra units below it.

Where it is useful

  • Checking airline baggage limits that are quoted in pounds.
  • Following fitness, diet or weightlifting plans written in lbs.
  • Reading product weights and shipping rates on foreign websites.
  • Understanding body-weight and baby-weight charts from another country.

The kg to lb formula

pounds = kilograms × 2.20462262185
kilograms = pounds ÷ 2.20462262185

One pound is defined as exactly 0.45359237 kg, which is where the factor comes from. A quick mental shortcut is to double the kilograms and add 10% of that number.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Round only at the end. Rounding the factor to 2.2 is fine for a quick estimate but drifts on large weights.
  • Check the direction before converting. Mixing up the two is the most common mistake.
  • Stone is used mainly in the UK and Ireland for body weight. One stone is 14 pounds.
  • Luggage scales often round up, so leave a small safety margin below the limit.

Frequently asked questions

How many pounds are in 1 kg?

1 kilogram is about 2.2046 pounds.

How do I convert lbs to kg?

Divide the pounds by 2.20462, or multiply by 0.45359237.

How many kg is 150 lbs?

150 lbs is about 68.04 kg.

What is the difference between lb and lbs?

Lb is the unit symbol and lbs is the common plural. Both mean pounds.

Is this converter exact?

It uses the exact definition of the pound. Displayed results are rounded for readability.
