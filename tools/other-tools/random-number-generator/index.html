About the Random Number Generator

The Random Number Generator picks numbers within the minimum and maximum you set. Choose how many numbers you need and whether duplicates are allowed. It is useful for games, giveaways, raffles, sampling and decision making.

How to use

  1. Enter the minimum and maximum numbers.
  2. Enter how many numbers you want and choose whether duplicates are allowed.
  3. Click Generate Random Numbers, then Copy if needed.

Where it is useful

  • Picking numbers for games and draws.
  • Choosing a random sample for tests and surveys.
  • Making decisions fairly.
  • Generating test data for practice.

How it works

The tool picks numbers within the minimum and maximum you set. With duplicates off, each number can appear only once, so the quantity cannot be bigger than the range. The numbers come from your browser's random number generator and are suitable for everyday use but not for security or cryptography.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Include both end values when you define the range.
  • Turn off duplicates for lottery-style draws.
  • Write down the result at once, as each click gives a new set.
  • For passwords use the Password Generator instead.

Frequently asked questions

Are the numbers truly random?

They come from your browser's random number generator, which is fine for games and everyday use but should not be used for security or cryptography.

How do I avoid repeated numbers?

Turn off Allow duplicate numbers. The quantity cannot be larger than the size of the range.

Can I use it for a giveaway?

Yes, for informal draws. For official or legal contests, follow the rules that apply to you.

Are the numbers truly random?

They are pseudo-random, which is fine for games and quick decisions.

Can I get decimals?

The tool generates whole numbers.
