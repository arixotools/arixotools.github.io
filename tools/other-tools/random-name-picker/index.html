Random Name Picker

Paste your names and pick one or more winners at random.

About the Random Name Picker

Whether you are running a social media giveaway, choosing who answers next in class, forming teams or deciding who pays for lunch, a random picker removes arguments about fairness. Paste your list of names, choose how many winners you want, and the tool draws them using your browser's secure random generator. Nobody is picked twice in the same draw.

How to use the Random Name Picker

  1. Type or paste the names, one per line.
  2. Choose how many winners to pick.
  3. Decide whether duplicate names should stay or be removed.
  4. Click Pick winners. Click again to draw a new result.

Where it is useful

  • Giveaways and lucky draws.
  • Classroom activities and presentations.
  • Choosing teams and order of turns.
  • Deciding who goes first, who pays or who cleans.

How the draw works

Each pick uses your browser's cryptographic random number generator, so every entry has an equal chance. After each pick the winner is removed from the pool, so the next pick is made from the remaining names. If you add a name twice and keep duplicates, that person has two chances.

Running a fair giveaway

  • Announce the rules and the end time before you start.
  • Collect entries in a list you can show others.
  • Consider recording your screen when you draw.
  • Check local laws and platform rules for giveaways and contests.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Remove duplicates if each person should have only one entry.
  • Each click produces a new random draw, so decide in advance whether you will redraw.
  • For teams, pick winners repeatedly or use the count to split your group.
  • This is for casual draws. Official lotteries need an audited process.

Frequently asked questions

Is the pick truly random?

It uses your browser's secure random generator, which is suitable for casual draws.

Can the same name win twice?

Not in the same draw, unless you entered that name twice and kept duplicates.

How many names can I add?

As many as you like, though very long lists may be slower.

Can I use this for a giveaway?

Yes, for informal ones. Follow your platform's and your local rules.

Is my list stored?

No. It stays in your browser.
