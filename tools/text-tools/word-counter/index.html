About the Word Counter

The Word Counter shows the number of words, characters and spaces in your text as you type. Writers, students and bloggers use it to meet essay limits, article lengths and social media rules.

How to use

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Read the live word, character and space counts.
  3. Click Clear to start again.

Frequently asked questions

How are words counted?

Words are groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks.

Is there a word limit?

There is no fixed limit, but very large texts may be slow to process in your browser.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Counting happens in your browser.
