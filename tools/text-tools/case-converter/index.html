About the Case Converter

The Case Converter changes the letter case of your text instantly. Convert to uppercase, lowercase, title case or sentence case without retyping. It is useful for headings, emails, social media posts and cleaning up copied text.

How to use

  1. Paste or type your text.
  2. Click UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case.
  3. Copy the converted text.

Frequently asked questions

What is title case?

Title case capitalises the first letter of each word, as in a heading.

What is sentence case?

Sentence case capitalises only the first letter of each sentence, like normal writing.

Is my text saved?

No. The text stays in your browser.
