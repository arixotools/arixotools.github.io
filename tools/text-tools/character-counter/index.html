About the Character Counter

The Character Counter shows how long your text is as you type. It counts characters, letters, numbers and spaces, which helps when you write for limits such as social media posts, SMS, meta descriptions or forms.

How to use

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Read the live counts shown on the page.
  3. Click Clear to start again.

Where it is useful

  • Staying within limits for SMS, social posts and bios.
  • Writing meta titles and descriptions of the right length.
  • Checking form fields with character limits.
  • Counting characters with and without spaces.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Emojis and some symbols may count as more than one character on some platforms.
  • Line breaks count as characters.
  • Spaces count unless you read the no-spaces figure.
  • Use the Word Counter when the limit is in words.

Frequently asked questions

Do spaces count as characters?

Yes, spaces are characters. The tool also shows counts that let you see the effect of spaces.

Why do my counts differ from another tool?

Tools may treat line breaks, emojis and special characters differently.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Counting happens in your browser.

Do spaces count as characters?

Yes, unless you look at the count without spaces.

Why does a platform show a different number?

Platforms may count emojis and line breaks differently.
