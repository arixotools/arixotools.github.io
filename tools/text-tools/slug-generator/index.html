Slug Generator

Convert a title into a clean URL slug as you type.

About the Slug Generator

A slug is the readable last part of a web address, such as easy-chicken-curry in a recipe URL. Clean slugs help people understand a link before they click it, and search engines use them as a small hint about the page. This generator turns any title into a tidy slug as you type: it lowercases the text, removes accents and punctuation, and joins the words with hyphens.

How to use the Slug Generator

  1. Type or paste the title of your page or post.
  2. Choose a hyphen or underscore, and an optional length limit.
  3. Tick Keep non-English letters if your URL should keep Bangla or Hindi letters.
  4. Copy the slug into your CMS or website.

Where it is useful

  • Writing permalinks for blog posts and product pages.
  • Naming files and folders consistently.
  • Creating IDs for categories and tags.
  • Cleaning titles that contain symbols and accents.

Slug best practices

  • Use hyphens, not underscores, between words. Search engines read hyphens as word separators.
  • Keep slugs short and descriptive, ideally under about 60 characters.
  • Use lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens only.
  • Drop filler words such as 'a', 'the' or 'and' when the meaning stays clear.
  • Avoid changing a slug after the page is live, or add a redirect from the old one.

Example

"Café au Lait — 10 Easy Recipes!" becomes cafe-au-lait-10-easy-recipes. Accented letters are converted to plain ones, the dash and exclamation mark are removed, and spaces become hyphens.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Turn on Keep non-English letters only if your site and server handle them well.
  • Use a length limit for readability, and the tool trims at a word boundary.
  • Do not include dates in slugs unless the date truly matters.
  • Check for an existing page with the same slug before publishing.

Frequently asked questions

What is a URL slug?

It is the human-readable end of a web address that identifies a page, like /my-first-post.

Should slugs use hyphens or underscores?

Hyphens are generally recommended for readability and SEO.

How long should a slug be?

Short and descriptive. Under about 60 characters is a good guide.

Can I keep Bangla letters in a slug?

Yes, tick 'Keep non-English letters', but plain English slugs are often more compatible.

Does the slug affect rankings?

It is a minor hint at most. Good content and links matter far more.
