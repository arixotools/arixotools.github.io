Number to Words Converter

Write numbers in words, in lakh-crore and million-billion styles, with a rupees format.

Up to 15 digits, with up to 2 decimal places. Commas are fine.

About the Number to Words Converter

Cheques, invoices, loan documents and legal papers often need an amount written in words as well as figures. This converter writes any number in words in both the Indian numbering system (thousand, lakh, crore) and the international system (thousand, million, billion). It also gives a ready rupees-and-paise sentence ending with "Only", the style normally used on cheques and bills.

How to use the Number to Words Converter

  1. Type or paste the number. Commas and the rupee sign are ignored.
  2. Add up to two decimal places if you need paise.
  3. Read the Indian, international and rupees versions as you type.
  4. Copy the text you need into your document.

Where it is useful

  • Writing the amount in words on cheques, receipts and invoices.
  • Drafting agreements, affidavits and loan paperwork.
  • Teaching and learning large numbers.
  • Checking that figures and words match in a document.

Indian versus international system

NumberIndian systemInternational system
1,00,000One LakhOne Hundred Thousand
12,34,567Twelve Lakh Thirty Four Thousand Five Hundred Sixty SevenOne Million Two Hundred Thirty Four Thousand Five Hundred Sixty Seven
1,00,00,000One CroreTen Million

The Indian system groups digits as 3, then 2, 2, 2 from the right. The international system groups them in threes.

Rupees format

For ₹105.50 the rupees format reads "One Hundred Five Rupees and Fifty Paise Only". Always read the final text against the figures before signing or submitting a document.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Check every word against your figures for official documents.
  • Decimals beyond two places are not accepted, as paise stop at two digits.
  • Many banks prefer the word 'Only' at the end to prevent additions.
  • Large numbers read best when you group the digits as you type.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1 crore in words?

One Crore in the Indian system, which is ten million in the international system.

How do I write 1,00,000 in words?

One Lakh, or one hundred thousand.

How do I write an amount in words on a cheque?

Use the rupees format, ending with Only, for example 'Five Thousand Rupees Only'.

Does it handle paise?

Yes, up to two decimal places.

What is the largest number supported?

Up to 15 digits before the decimal point.
