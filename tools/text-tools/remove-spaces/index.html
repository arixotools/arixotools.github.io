About the Remove Spaces

The Remove Spaces tool cleans up text in one click. Remove every space, reduce repeated spaces to a single space, or strip line breaks. It is useful for cleaning copied text, preparing data and tidying messy documents.

How to use

  1. Paste your text into the box.
  2. Click Remove All Spaces, Remove Extra Spaces or Remove Line Breaks.
  3. Copy the cleaned text.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the options?

Remove All Spaces deletes every space. Remove Extra Spaces keeps single spaces between words but removes repeats. Remove Line Breaks joins the text into one line.

Will it change my words?

No. Only spaces and line breaks are changed.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
