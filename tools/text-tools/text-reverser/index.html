About the Text Reverser

The Text Reverser flips your text instantly. Reverse the whole text character by character, or keep the words readable and reverse only the order of the words. It is fun for puzzles and handy for quick text checks.

How to use

  1. Type or paste your text.
  2. Click Reverse Text for characters or Reverse Words for word order.
  3. Copy the result.

Where it is useful

  • Making puzzles and word games.
  • Checking whether a word is a palindrome.
  • Creating mirrored text for fun.
  • Testing text handling in programs.

Palindrome check

A palindrome reads the same backwards, such as level or madam. Reverse the text and compare it to the original. For sentences, ignore spaces and punctuation before comparing.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Reverse Text flips every character, while Reverse Words keeps each word and flips their order.
  • Reversing punctuation moves it to the other end.
  • Complex scripts with joined letters may reverse awkwardly.
  • Copy the result straight away.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the two buttons?

Reverse Text flips every character, so the last letter comes first. Reverse Words keeps each word intact but reverses their order.

Can I use it to check palindromes?

Yes. If the reversed text matches the original, ignoring spaces and punctuation, it is a palindrome.

Is my text saved?

No. It stays in your browser.

What is the difference between the buttons?

One reverses characters, the other reverses word order.

Does it work with other languages?

It works on any text, but joined scripts may look odd.
