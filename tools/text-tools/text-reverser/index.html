About the Text Reverser

The Text Reverser flips your text instantly. Reverse the whole text character by character, or keep the words readable and reverse only the order of the words. It is fun for puzzles and handy for quick text checks.

How to use

  1. Type or paste your text.
  2. Click Reverse Text for characters or Reverse Words for word order.
  3. Copy the result.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the two buttons?

Reverse Text flips every character, so the last letter comes first. Reverse Words keeps each word intact but reverses their order.

Can I use it to check palindromes?

Yes. If the reversed text matches the original, ignoring spaces and punctuation, it is a palindrome.

Is my text saved?

No. It stays in your browser.
