How to Use the Percentage Calculator

ARIXO's Percentage Calculator helps you solve common percentage problems quickly without doing manual calculations.

You can calculate a percentage of a number, find what percentage one number represents of another number, and calculate percentage increase or decrease.

Percentage Calculation Examples

To calculate a percentage of a number, use the basic formula: percentage × number ÷ 100.

For example, to find 20% of 200, enter 20 as the percentage and 200 as the number. The calculator will provide the result instantly.

To find what percentage one number is of another, divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100.

To calculate percentage increase or decrease, enter the original value and the new value. The calculator will determine the percentage change.
