How to Use the BMI Calculator

ARIXO's BMI Calculator helps you calculate Body Mass Index using your height and weight. Enter your height in centimeters and your weight in kilograms, then select Calculate BMI.

The calculator displays your BMI value and the corresponding general adult BMI category.

How Is BMI Calculated?

BMI is calculated by dividing body weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

For example, a person weighing 65 kg with a height of 1.70 meters would have a BMI of approximately 22.5.

Adult BMI Categories

  • Underweight: Below 18.5
  • Normal weight: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25 – 29.9
  • Obesity: 30 or higher

Is BMI a Complete Measure of Health?

BMI is a general screening measure and should not be used by itself to diagnose a health condition. It does not directly measure body fat and may not accurately represent every person. Age, sex, muscle mass and other factors can affect how BMI should be interpreted.

This calculator is intended for general informational purposes. For personal health advice or medical decisions, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
