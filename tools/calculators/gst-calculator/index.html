About the GST Calculator

ARIXO's GST Calculator helps you work out the Goods and Services Tax on any amount. You can add GST to a price (GST exclusive) or find the original price from a GST-inclusive total, and see the CGST and SGST split. Since 22 September 2025 most goods and services in India fall under 5% or 18%, with 40% for specified luxury and sin goods and 3% for gold and silver. The older 12% and 28% rates are kept only for checking past invoices.

How to use

  1. Enter the amount in rupees.
  2. Choose the GST rate that applies to your item.
  3. Select GST Exclusive (add GST) or GST Inclusive (remove GST), then click Calculate GST.

Where it is useful

  • Preparing invoices and bills with the correct GST amount.
  • Finding the base price when a shop quotes a GST-inclusive total.
  • Checking the tax portion of a purchase for expense claims.
  • Comparing prices of goods that fall in different GST slabs.

Worked example

An item costs ₹10,000 before tax and the GST rate is 18%. GST is 10,000 × 18% = ₹1,800, so the total is ₹11,800, with ₹900 as CGST and ₹900 as SGST. In the other direction, if a bill shows ₹11,800 including 18% GST, the base price is 11,800 ÷ 1.18 = ₹10,000.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Always confirm the current rate for your product, because rates and classifications change.
  • Use GST Exclusive when you know the price before tax, and GST Inclusive when the price already includes tax.
  • For sales between states, IGST applies instead of CGST plus SGST, but the total rate is the same.
  • Keep invoices and rounding consistent, because small paise differences can add up on large orders.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between GST exclusive and inclusive?

Exclusive means GST is added on top of the amount you enter. Inclusive means the amount already contains GST and the calculator works backwards to find the base price.

What are CGST and SGST?

For sales within one state, the GST is split equally between the Central GST and the State GST. At 18%, for example, CGST is 9% and SGST is 9%.

Which GST rate should I choose?

The rate depends on how your product or service is classified. Always confirm the current rate for your item from the official GST rate schedule or your accountant before invoicing.

Is GST calculated on the discounted price?

In general GST is charged on the transaction value after any discount shown on the invoice, but check the rules for your case.

What is IGST?

Integrated GST applies to sales between different states and equals CGST plus SGST combined.
