How to Use the Loan Calculator

ARIXO's Loan Calculator helps you estimate your monthly loan payment, total interest and total repayment. Enter your loan details and select Calculate Loan.

  • Enter the loan amount.
  • Enter the annual interest rate.
  • Enter the loan tenure in years.
  • Click Calculate Loan.
  • View the estimated monthly payment and total repayment.

Loan Payment Formula

For a standard reducing-balance loan, the monthly payment can be calculated using the principal amount, monthly interest rate and total number of monthly payments.

Payment = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

Here, P is the principal loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the total number of monthly payments.

Why Use a Loan Calculator?

A loan calculator can help you understand the approximate cost of borrowing before applying for a loan. You can compare different loan amounts, interest rates and repayment periods.

  • Estimate your monthly loan payment.
  • Understand the approximate interest cost.
  • Estimate the total repayment amount.
  • Compare different loan tenures.
  • Plan your monthly budget.

Types of Loans You Can Estimate

Loan calculations can be useful for personal loans, car loans, home loans, education loans and other loans that use a reducing-balance repayment method.
