About the Unit Converter

ARIXO's Unit Converter turns one unit into another in seconds. It covers length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed and time. Choose a category, enter a value, pick the From and To units, and convert. The swap button reverses the units in one click.

How to use

  1. Select a category such as Length or Weight.
  2. Enter the value and choose the From and To units.
  3. Click Convert, or use Swap Units to reverse the conversion.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius value by 9/5 and add 32. Choose the Temperature category and the calculator does this for you.

Are the results exact?

Conversions use standard conversion factors. Very long decimals may be rounded for readability.

Which categories are supported?

Length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed and time.
