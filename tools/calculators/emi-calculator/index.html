EMI Formula

The standard reducing-balance EMI formula is based on the loan principal, monthly interest rate and total number of monthly payments.

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

Here, P represents the loan principal, r represents the monthly interest rate and n represents the total number of monthly payments.

Why Use an EMI Calculator?

An EMI calculator can help you estimate your monthly repayment before applying for a loan. It can also help you understand the approximate interest cost and total amount payable over the selected loan tenure.

  • Estimate monthly loan payments.
  • Understand approximate interest costs.
  • Compare different loan amounts and tenures.
  • Plan your monthly budget more easily.
  • Estimate repayments for different types of loans.

Where Can You Use an EMI Calculator?

EMI calculations are commonly useful for personal loans, home loans, car loans, education loans and other reducing-balance loan calculations.
