About the Age Calculator

The Age Calculator works out your exact age from your date of birth, in years, months and days. It is useful for forms, school and job applications, eligibility checks and simple curiosity. Pick your date of birth, click Calculate and the result appears instantly. It uses today's date from your device.

How to use

  1. Select your date of birth.
  2. Click Calculate.
  3. Read your age in years, months and days.

Frequently asked questions

How is age calculated?

The calculator subtracts your date of birth from today's date and shows the difference in years, months and days.

Can I calculate the age between two other dates?

Yes. Use the Age Difference Calculator to find the gap between any two dates.

Is my date of birth saved?

No. The calculation happens in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
