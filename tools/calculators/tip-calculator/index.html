About the Tip Calculator

The Tip Calculator works out how much tip to leave and how to split the bill fairly. Enter the bill amount, choose your tip percentage and the number of people, and you will see the tip, the total and each person's share. It is handy for restaurants, cafes, cabs and group outings.

How to use

  1. Enter the bill amount.
  2. Enter the tip percentage you want to give.
  3. Enter the number of people and click Calculate Tip.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I tip?

There is no fixed rule in India. Many people leave around 5% to 10% at restaurants when no service charge is added. Check your bill first, because some places already include a service charge.

Is the tip calculated before or after tax?

That is up to you. This calculator applies the tip percentage to the bill amount you enter, so enter the pre-tax or post-tax amount as you prefer.

Can I split the bill unevenly?

This tool splits the total equally. For uneven splits, calculate each person's share of the bill first and then add the tip on top.
