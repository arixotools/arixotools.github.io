Simple Interest Formula

Simple interest is calculated using the original principal amount throughout the entire period.

SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100 P = Principal Amount
R = Annual Interest Rate
T = Time in Years

The total amount is calculated as:

Total Amount = P + SI
