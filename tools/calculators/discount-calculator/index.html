How to Use the Discount Calculator

ARIXO's Discount Calculator helps you quickly find the discount amount, final price and total savings when an item is offered at a percentage discount.

Enter the original price of the product and the discount percentage. Then click the Calculate Discount button to see the discount amount, final price and money saved.

How to Calculate a Discount

The discount amount can be calculated by multiplying the original price by the discount percentage and dividing the result by 100.

For example, if an item costs ₹1,000 and has a 20% discount, the discount amount is ₹200 and the final price is ₹800.

What Does the Discount Calculator Show?

  • Discount Amount: The amount reduced from the original price.
  • Final Price: The price you pay after the discount.
  • You Save: The total amount saved from the original price.
