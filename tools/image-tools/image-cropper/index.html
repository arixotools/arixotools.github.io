About the Image Cropper

The Image Cropper lets you cut away the parts of a picture you do not need. Load your image, select the area to keep, and crop. The work happens in your browser, so your photo stays on your device.

How to use

  1. Click Choose Image and select your picture.
  2. Select the area you want to keep.
  3. Click Crop Image and download the result.

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping reduce quality?

Cropping removes pixels outside the selection. The pixels you keep stay the same, but the image becomes smaller.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Cropping is done in your browser.

Can I crop to a fixed size?

To get an exact size, crop first and then use the Image Resizer.
