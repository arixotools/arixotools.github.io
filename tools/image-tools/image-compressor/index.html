About the Image Compressor

The Image Compressor reduces the file size of your pictures so they upload faster and use less storage. Choose a quality level, compress, and download. Your image is processed inside your browser and is not uploaded to our servers.

How to use

  1. Click Choose Image and select your picture.
  2. Adjust the compression quality slider.
  3. Click Compress Image and download the result.

Frequently asked questions

Will compression reduce image quality?

Lower quality settings make smaller files but can add visible blur or artefacts. A setting around 70% to 80% is a good balance for most photos.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The image is processed in your browser.

Which formats work best?

Photos compress best. Images that are already small or heavily compressed may not shrink much.
