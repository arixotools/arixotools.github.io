About the Image Resizer

The Image Resizer changes the width and height of your image in pixels. Keep the aspect ratio locked to avoid stretching, or turn it off for an exact size. Your picture is processed in your browser and is not uploaded.

How to use

  1. Click Choose Image and select your picture.
  2. Enter the new width and height, and keep Aspect ratio on to avoid distortion.
  3. Click Resize Image and download the result.

Frequently asked questions

What is aspect ratio?

It is the proportion between width and height. Keeping it locked means changing one value updates the other so the picture is not stretched.

Does enlarging an image reduce quality?

Yes. Making an image larger than the original can make it look soft or pixelated.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Resizing happens in your browser.
