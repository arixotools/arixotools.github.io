About the Image to JPG

The Image to JPG converter changes your picture into the widely supported JPG format. JPG files are usually smaller than PNG files, which makes them good for sharing and uploading. Set the quality, convert and download. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

How to use

  1. Click Choose Image and select your picture.
  2. Adjust the JPG quality slider.
  3. Click Convert to JPG and download the file.

Frequently asked questions

Why convert an image to JPG?

JPG is accepted almost everywhere and usually gives smaller files for photos.

What happens to transparent areas?

JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas are filled with a solid colour.

Is JPG quality lost?

JPG uses lossy compression. A higher quality setting keeps more detail but gives a larger file.
