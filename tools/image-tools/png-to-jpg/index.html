About the PNG to JPG Converter

PNG files keep every detail and transparency, which makes them large, especially for photos and screenshots. When you need a lighter file for email, a job application or a website, a JPG is usually far smaller. This converter changes PNG images into JPG with a quality slider you control. Everything happens in your browser, and your files are not uploaded.

How to use the PNG to JPG Converter

  1. Click Choose image files and select your PNG images.
  2. Set the quality. Around 90% is a good balance for photos.
  3. Choose the background color that will replace any transparent areas.
  4. Click Convert to JPG and download the new files.

Where it is useful

  • Making large screenshots and photos small enough to email.
  • Meeting file size limits on online forms.
  • Saving storage space and speeding up web pages.
  • Converting graphics for apps that accept only JPG.

Choosing quality

At 90% quality a photo usually looks the same as the original but can be several times smaller. At 60% you will often see blockiness. For screenshots with sharp text, JPG can blur the edges, so test one image first.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Pick white as the background for most documents, or a color that matches your page.
  • JPG is best for photos. For logos and sharp graphics, keep PNG.
  • Do not convert back and forth between formats, because each lossy step lowers quality.
  • Check the result size shown for each image before downloading.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert PNG to JPG?

Choose your PNG files, set the quality, click Convert to JPG and download.

Why is the JPG smaller than the PNG?

JPG uses lossy compression that discards small details your eyes barely notice.

What happens to a transparent background?

It is filled with the color you choose, because JPG has no transparency.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Conversion happens in your browser.

Is there a size limit?

Very large images may be too heavy for your browser. Up to 30 files can be converted at once.
