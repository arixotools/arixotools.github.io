About the JPG to PNG

The JPG to PNG converter turns JPG and JPEG pictures into PNG files. PNG is lossless, so it is useful when you want to edit an image repeatedly without further compression loss. The conversion runs in your browser and your image is not uploaded.

How to use

  1. Click Choose JPG Image and select your file.
  2. Click Convert to PNG.
  3. Download the PNG file.

Frequently asked questions

Does converting JPG to PNG improve quality?

No. The PNG keeps the quality of the JPG but cannot restore detail that was already lost.

Why is the PNG file bigger?

PNG is lossless, so photo files are often larger than JPG files.

Is my image uploaded?

No. The conversion happens in your browser.
