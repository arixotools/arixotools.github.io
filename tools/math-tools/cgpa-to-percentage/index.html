Convert a 10-point CGPA to a percentage, or a percentage back to CGPA.

About the CGPA to Percentage Converter

Many schools and colleges in India grade students on a 10-point CGPA scale, while job applications, exam forms and admissions often ask for a percentage. The common conversion is the CBSE method: multiply CGPA by 9.5 to get an approximate percentage. This converter applies that formula in both directions.

How to use the CGPA to Percentage Converter

  1. Choose CGPA to percentage or Percentage to CGPA.
  2. Enter your CGPA (up to 10) or your percentage.
  3. Click Convert.
  4. Read the result.

Where it is useful

  • Filling job or admission forms that ask for a percentage.
  • Applying for higher studies or government jobs with percentage cut-offs.
  • Comparing results across boards and universities.
  • Checking how close your CGPA is to a target percentage.

Formula and examples

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5
CGPA = Percentage ÷ 9.5

A CGPA of 8.4 is 79.8%, and 9.0 is 85.5%. A CGPA of 7.0 is 66.5%.

Important

The 9.5 multiplier is the method CBSE uses for its own 10-point grading. Other boards and universities use their own formulas and scales, and some give an official conversion certificate. For official purposes, use the conversion your institution specifies.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Check which formula your university uses before submitting a converted figure.
  • Do not use this for 4-point or 5-point GPA scales.
  • Ask your school or college for a CGPA-to-percentage certificate when a form requires it.
  • Round only at the end if you are adding or averaging several results.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert CGPA to percentage?

Multiply your CGPA by 9.5. For example, 8.2 × 9.5 = 77.9%.

Is the 9.5 formula official?

It is the CBSE method. Other universities may use different rules.

How do I convert percentage to CGPA?

Divide the percentage by 9.5.

Does this work for a 4-point GPA?

No. This tool is for the 10-point CGPA scale.

Which CGPA equals 75%?

75 ÷ 9.5 is about 7.89 CGPA.
