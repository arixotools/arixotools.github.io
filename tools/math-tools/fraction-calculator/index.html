Fraction Calculator

Add, subtract, multiply or divide two fractions with a simplified result.

About the Fraction Calculator

Fractions are easy to get wrong by hand because adding or subtracting needs a common denominator, and the answer should then be simplified. This calculator handles all four operations on two fractions and always gives the answer in simplest form, as a mixed number where that applies, and as a decimal.

How to use the Fraction Calculator

  1. Enter the numerator and denominator of the first fraction.
  2. Choose add, subtract, multiply or divide.
  3. Enter the second fraction.
  4. Click Calculate.

Where it is useful

  • Checking school homework on fractions.
  • Scaling recipes and measurements.
  • Carpentry, sewing and DIY measurements.
  • Simplifying ratios and probabilities.

Rules used

OperationRule
Adda/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd
Subtracta/b − c/d = (ad − bc) / bd
Multiplya/b × c/d = ac / bd
Dividea/b ÷ c/d = ad / bc

After each operation, the result is divided by the highest common factor of the numerator and denominator to reach simplest form. For example, 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6 and 3/4 ÷ 3/8 = 2.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Enter negatives in the numerator, for example −1 over 2.
  • A zero denominator is not allowed.
  • Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal.
  • Use the HCF and LCM calculator to see the common denominator.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add fractions?

Find a common denominator, add the numerators, then simplify. This tool does it for you.

How do I simplify a fraction?

Divide the numerator and denominator by their highest common factor.

What is a mixed number?

A whole number with a proper fraction, such as 1 3/4.

How do I divide fractions?

Multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second.

Can I use negative fractions?

Yes. Put the minus sign in the numerator.
