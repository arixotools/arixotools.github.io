About the Average Calculator

The average, or arithmetic mean, is the sum of a list of numbers divided by how many there are. It is one of the most used numbers in school, work and daily life, from test marks to monthly expenses. This calculator also gives the median, mode, sum, count, minimum, maximum and range, so you can see the shape of your data and not only its mean.

How to use the Average Calculator

  1. Type or paste your numbers into the box. Spaces, commas, semicolons and new lines all work.
  2. Click Calculate.
  3. Read the mean first, then the median, mode and the other figures.
  4. Edit the list and calculate again as needed.

Where it is useful

  • Finding the average of marks, scores or expenses.
  • Checking whether a few extreme values are skewing an average, by comparing mean and median.
  • Quick statistics for homework and spreadsheets.
  • Finding the most common value in a list.

Mean, median and mode explained

MeasureWhat it isExample for 12, 15, 15, 18, 20
MeanSum ÷ count16
MedianThe middle value when sorted15
ModeThe most frequent value15
RangeLargest − smallest8

When a list has a few extreme values, the median often describes a typical value better than the mean. For example, in incomes, one very high salary pulls the mean up but barely moves the median.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Make sure there are no stray symbols. Percent signs and currency symbols cause an error.
  • If every number appears once there is no mode.
  • If two values tie for most frequent, both are shown as the mode.
  • For a weighted average, such as marks with different credits, multiply each value by its weight first.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate an average?

Add all the numbers and divide by how many numbers there are.

What is the difference between mean and median?

The mean is the sum divided by the count. The median is the middle value of the sorted list.

What is the mode?

The value that appears most often.

Can I enter decimals and negatives?

Yes, both are supported.

How many numbers can I enter?

There is no fixed limit, though very large lists may be slower.
