Convert between numbers and Roman numerals (1 to 3999).

About the Roman Numeral Converter

Roman numerals are still used on clocks, in book chapters, movie sequels, building cornerstones and for years such as MMXXIV. This converter works in both directions as you type. Enter a number from 1 to 3999 to get its Roman numeral, or enter Roman letters to get the number.

How to use the Roman Numeral Converter

  1. Type a number such as 2024, or a Roman numeral such as XLII.
  2. The result appears instantly.
  3. Edit the input to try another value.

Where it is useful

  • Writing dates, years and chapter numbers in Roman numerals.
  • Checking homework and quizzes.
  • Reading the year on monuments and old documents.
  • Designing tattoos, logos and invitations that use Roman numerals.

A smaller symbol before a larger one is subtracted (IV = 4, IX = 9, XL = 40, XC = 90, CD = 400, CM = 900). Otherwise symbols are added. A symbol is not repeated more than three times in a row, so 4 is IV and not IIII.

Examples: 2024 = MMXXIV, 1999 = MCMXCIX and 42 = XLII.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Roman numerals have no zero and no negative numbers.
  • The standard system stops at 3999, because 4000 would need a symbol that is not in common use.
  • The tool rejects non-standard forms such as IIII and shows the correct form.
  • Letters are case-insensitive.

Frequently asked questions

What is 2024 in Roman numerals?

2024 is MMXXIV.

What is the largest standard Roman numeral?

3999, written MMMCMXCIX.

Why is 4 written IV and not IIII?

Standard modern usage uses subtractive notation. Some clock faces still use IIII.

Is there a Roman numeral for zero?

No. The system has no zero.

Can I convert fractions?

No. This tool covers whole numbers from 1 to 3999.
